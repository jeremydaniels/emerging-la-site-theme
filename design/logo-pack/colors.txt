Emerging LA logo colors

Black   #000000   RGB 0 0 0
White   #FFFFFF   RGB 255 255 255
Cream   #F5F0EB   RGB 245 240 235
Orange  #FF4B33   RGB 255 75 51

Which file to use
SVG  web, Figma, Canva, most design tools
PDF  print, Illustrator, sending to printers
EPS  older print shops and sign makers
PNG  anywhere that won't take the files above (email, slides, social)

Transparent files have no background. Use these for flyers and partner logos.
